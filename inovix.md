# Build a Production-Ready Website for Inovixa Digital

You are a senior full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, SEO specialist, and conversion-focused web designer.

Build a complete, modern, production-ready website for a digital agency called:

# Inovixa Digital

Domain:

`inovixadigital.com`

The company specializes primarily in helping small and local businesses that:

* Do not have a website
* Have an old or outdated website
* Have poor mobile usability
* Have slow websites
* Have websites that do not generate leads
* Need website redesign
* Need SEO
* Need website maintenance
* Later may need web applications, automation, mobile apps, and AI integrations

The primary target market is initially:

* USA local businesses
* UK local businesses
* English-speaking international businesses

Primary target niches:

* Roofing companies
* Plumbing companies
* HVAC companies
* Dental clinics
* Auto repair shops
* Cleaning companies
* Landscaping businesses
* Construction companies
* Real estate businesses
* Accounting firms

The website should NOT feel like a generic outsourcing company.

It should feel like a:

* Premium small digital agency
* Modern website modernization company
* Conversion-focused web agency
* Reliable long-term digital partner
